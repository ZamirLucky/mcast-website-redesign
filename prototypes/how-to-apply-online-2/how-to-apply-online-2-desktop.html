Application Time Windows

Check the official 2026/2027 application windows for international applicants, Main Call applications, transfers, and late applications.

  • BSc (Hons) Nursing: international applications received until Thursday 30 April 2026

Form A - Active Valid EID Required

Applicants residing in Malta must use active and valid EID credentials to create their MCAST CMIS student profile and submit the required uploads.

  • Use an active and valid EID account before starting Form A.
  • Create or access your MCAST CMIS student profile.
  • Upload all required documents by the deadline for processing.

Need help with your application?

For help with your application, contact MCAST Admissions Support. When emailing, include your full name, surname, ID number, and enough details for the team to understand what assistance is required.

If in-person assistance is needed, applicants should email first to request an appointment. Where needed, support staff may assist online through AnyDesk during a support call.
