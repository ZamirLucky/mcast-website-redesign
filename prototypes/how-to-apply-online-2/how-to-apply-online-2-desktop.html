International Non-EU Applicants Not Yet Residing in Malta

Guidance for third-country applicants not yet residing in Malta and not entitled to active Maltese EID credentials.

expand_more

This online application window is for Third Country non-EU nationals not yet residing in Malta and therefore without an active and valid Maltese EID.

The online application portal is available until Tuesday 30 June 2026 for eligible Third Country non-EU nationals not yet residing in Malta.

Required uploads:

  • MQRIC statement/s for non-Maltese qualifications
  • Certificates and official transcripts/results in English, including required apostilled translations
  • Original English proficiency certificates
  • All other necessary documentation

Important: If required certification is not attained by the deadline, a maximum of 10 working days from the deadline may be granted to present missing documentation. Beyond this period, incomplete applications will not be processed for the October 2026 intake.

MQRIC: If MQRIC statements are required but not yet in possession by 30 June 2026, the applicant must present the official receipt from MQRIC.

For queries: applyinternational@mcast.edu.mt

Attention: Third Country Nationals on WBL Courses

Important guidance for TCN applicants considering courses with Work-Based Learning, placements, apprenticeships, or internships.

expand_more

Third-country national applicants considering a full-time programme that includes Work-Based Learning, an apprenticeship, placement, or internship must verify with the relevant national authorities that they are permitted to meet the mandatory attendance hours for the accredited WBL units.

This verification should include any legal or employment-related requirements, including necessary employment licensing fees, signing the MCAST Work-Based Learning Training Agreement, and registration with JobsPlus where applicable.

Warning: MCAST will not be held liable for financial repercussions arising from failing to complete the preparatory process of determining eligibility for any applicable WBL commitments related to the selected course.
