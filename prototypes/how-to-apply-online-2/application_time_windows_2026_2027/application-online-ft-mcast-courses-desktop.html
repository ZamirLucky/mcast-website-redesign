Important Information

Other Admissions Categories

These routes apply to progressing, continuing, repeating, and returning MCAST applicants. Some categories are normally guided by Institute communication after final or annual results are issued.

B

Form B — Progressing

Progressing from one level of study in 2025/2026 to the next level in 2026/2027.

C

Form C — Continuing

Continuing from one year of studies in 2025/2026 to the next year in 2026/2027.

D

Form D — Repeating

Repeating units of study by attendance and assessment where programme regulations allow this.

R

Form R — Returning on same course

Returning on the same course after leaving MCAST in previous years.

RC

Form RC — Returning onto a different course

Returning to MCAST onto a different course.

Important note

Forms B, C, and D are normally guided by Institute communication after final or annual results are issued. The deadline is Thursday 14 August 2026 at 1400h. Later submissions will not be considered unless exceptions are clearly communicated by the Institute.

Exceptions and late submissions

Exceptions to the normal Form B, C, and D timelines may only arise through clear communication from the Institutes, and only where results leading to the required forms have not been concluded or published for reasons such as CSR or WBL final results not being concluded, pending assignments, synoptic assessments not concluded, or similar exceptional cases.

Where results have been published and a clear Form B, C, or D application status has been assigned and communicated in time for the student to submit by Thursday 14 August 2026 at 1400h, failure to submit by the deadline means the student is no longer interested in progressing, continuing, or repeating.

Later submissions for such applications will not be considered.
