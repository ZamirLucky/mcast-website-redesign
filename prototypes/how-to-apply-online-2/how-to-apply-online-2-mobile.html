Applications are received online. Applicants using the Main Call must create an MCAST CMIS Student Profile using active and valid EID credentials and upload all required documentation.

Need help with your application?

For help with your application, contact MCAST Admissions Support. When emailing, include your full name, surname, ID number, and enough details for the team to understand what assistance is required.

Call times: Monday to Friday, 8am to 2pm. If in-person assistance is needed, applicants should email first to request an appointment. Where needed, support staff may assist online through AnyDesk during a support call.
