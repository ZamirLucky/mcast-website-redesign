• FAQ

Help Centre

Common questions from students, parents, and visitors.

Applications are typically opened in July for the October intake. You can apply directly via our online student portal using your e-ID credentials. Detailed step-by-step guides are available in the Admissions section.

Our full prospectus is available on the main website under the "Study" menu. You can browse courses by institute, level (MQF 1 to 7), or career path. Each course page includes entry requirements and learning outcomes.

The Admissions Office is located at the Main Campus in Paola. You can contact them via email at admissions@mcast.edu.mt or by calling +356 2398 7100 during standard office hours.

While Paola is the central hub, specific institutes are based in Mosta (Creative Arts), Qormi (Applied Sciences), and Għajnsielem (Gozo). Please check your specific course location before visiting for academic matters.
