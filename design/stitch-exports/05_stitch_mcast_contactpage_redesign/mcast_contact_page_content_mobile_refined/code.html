Help Centre

Common questions from students, parents, and visitors.

How do I apply for a course? add
Applications for most MCAST courses are submitted online through our official portal during the intake periods. You'll need to create an account, upload your qualifications, and follow the step-by-step instructions.
Where can I find course information? add
Full course descriptions, entry requirements, and module details are available on the "Our Courses" section of the MCAST website. You can also visit our information office on campus.
Which campus should I visit? add
This depends on your field of study. Arts and Media are primarily based in Mosta, while technical and scientific subjects are located at our Paola main campus. Check your specific course details for the designated campus location.
