Frequently Asked Questions

Applications are submitted online via our admissions portal during the intake period. You will need to provide digital copies of your qualifications and identification documents.

Prospective students can book a one-on-one session with our Career Guidance team to discuss course options and career paths. Sessions can be booked through our website.

Requirements vary by course level. Foundation courses typically require school-leaving certificates, while degree courses require specific A-Level or Diploma qualifications.

Most MCAST students are eligible for stipends and grants through the national student support scheme. Specialized scholarships are also available for specific programs.

Current students can reach out to the IT helpdesk via the student portal or by emailing itsupport@mcast.edu.mt for issues related to accounts, Moodle, or campus Wi-Fi.
